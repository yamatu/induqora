## Reading FANUC part numbers correctly

Most mistakes in FANUC parts buying come from mismatched part numbers. FANUC's numbering follows a recognizable pattern:

| Prefix | Typical product group |
|---|---|
| A02B | CNC control units |
| A03B | I/O modules |
| A06B | Servo and spindle motors (A06B-0…) and amplifiers / power supplies (A06B-6…) |
| A16B / A20B | Printed circuit boards |

Always compare the **complete** number on the nameplate of the failed unit — including the final suffix or revision letter — with the listing. Two amplifiers that differ only in the suffix can differ in current rating, interface or firmware compatibility. Vcocnc's product pages make the same recommendation.

## Testing, repair and refurbishment

Vcocnc presents its business as **"sales, testing and maintenance"** handled as one service: part-number checks, sourcing, testing, repair evaluation and international shipping. According to the company:

- **Incoming inspection and functional checks** are carried out in the Kunshan workshop before dispatch.
- **Repair and refurbishment** is offered for FANUC components such as amplifiers, motors and boards, with warranty included on completed work.
- **Technical support** helps customers confirm part numbers and interchangeability before ordering.

Repair is often the faster and cheaper route for legacy FANUC amplifiers and boards whose replacement units are scarce. When requesting a repair quote from any supplier, send the full nameplate number, the alarm code shown on the CNC, the machine and control model, and photos of the unit and its connectors.

## Who Vcocnc suits — and what to check

Vcocnc is a practical option for:

- **Maintenance teams running FANUC-controlled machine tools**, especially older 0i, 16i/18i and 30i-generation equipment where replacement drives are hard to source.
- **Machine builders and integrators** who need a single supplier for mixed-brand spare parts.
- **Buyers outside China** looking for surplus and obsolete stock with tracked international shipping.

As with any independent (non-OEM) parts supplier, confirm before ordering:

1. The **exact part number and suffix**, matched to your nameplate.
2. The **condition** of the specific unit — new, new surplus, used or refurbished.
3. Whether the unit has been **functionally tested**, and how.
4. **Warranty terms and return conditions** in writing on the quotation.
5. **Lead time and shipping method**, especially if the machine is down.
