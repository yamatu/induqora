*Company profile. This article describes Vibocnc based on the information the company publishes on [vibocnc.com](https://vibocnc.com/), reviewed by Induqora in November 2026. Figures such as staff numbers and stock levels are the company's own statements and have not been independently audited.*

**Vibocnc** describes itself as a **CNC parts center** for industrial maintenance teams. Operating from **Kunshan, Jiangsu Province** since **2007**, it combines a very large multi-brand catalog of automation spare parts with a structured **repair service** for servo drives, HMIs, PLC modules, control boards and other industrial electronics — and ships to customers in more than 50 countries.

Where many spare-parts sellers stop at sourcing, Vibocnc's site is organized around both questions a maintenance engineer asks when a machine is down: *can I buy a replacement today, and can the failed unit be repaired?*

### How the repair process works

1. **Submit unit details** — brand, exact model, fault symptoms, quantity and urgency (standard evaluation, production stopped, or planned maintenance).
2. **Initial evaluation** — a **free initial review** based on the information provided, confirming whether repair or replacement is the better option.
3. **Approval and shipment** — if physical inspection is needed, Vibocnc issues a repair reference and packing instructions. Units should not be shipped before this.
4. **Diagnosis and repair** — after inspection and approval, the work is completed and the unit is functionally checked where test support exists.
5. **Return shipment** — the repaired unit is packed for export and returned with tracking information.

If a repair is not practical, Vibocnc checks its replacement stock or compatible sourcing options — useful when the failed unit is beyond economic repair.

### What to send with a repair request

The quality of the first diagnosis depends on the information supplied. Vibocnc asks for:

- The exact brand, model and **full nameplate number**
- **Alarm codes** and a clear description of the failure
- The **machine, controller or application** where the unit is installed
- **Photos** of the nameplate, connectors, front and rear of the unit and any visible damage
- Required quantity and whether **production is stopped**

## Warranty, returns and shipping

- **Warranty:** standard coverage is **12 months** unless the quotation or product page states otherwise, covering verified functional defects under normal operating conditions. Exclusions include incorrect installation or wiring, physical or environmental damage (moisture, surge, overheating), unauthorized repair or modification, and failures elsewhere in the machine. Details are in the [warranty policy](https://vibocnc.com/warranty-policy).
- **Claims:** customers provide the order number, SKU, serial number, fault description and photos or video; return instructions are issued before anything is shipped back.
- **Returns:** product pages state that returns are accepted within one year, with return shipping costs shared.
- **Shipping:** DHL, FedEx or UPS, with typical transit of 4–5 days depending on destination and customs.
- **Part verification:** before dispatch, Vibocnc checks interchangeability when customers send the machine builder, controller model, drive reference and alarm code.
- **Payment:** PayPal is listed on the site.
